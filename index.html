Ballin Roller

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Ballin Roller is an offline dice roller for tabletop role-playing games. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: nothing. The app collects no data, shares no data, and sends nothing anywhere.

Information the app collects

None. Ballin Roller does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device information, location data, or usage data. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Roll history

Your roll history is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage, so you can see your recent rolls. It never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the CLEAR button in the app, and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

Third parties and sharing

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking tools, and no third-party services or software development kits. No data is shared with any other party, because no data is collected in the first place.

Network access

The app does not access the internet and requests no network permission. It works fully offline.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
