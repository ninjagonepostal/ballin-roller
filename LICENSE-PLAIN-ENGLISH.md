## The short version

Ballin' Roller is free software. "Free" here means freedom, not price: you
are free to use it, copy it, share it, and change it. The one big rule is
that you have to pass those same freedoms on to everyone else.

- **Use it** for anything you want, including commercial work.
- **Copy it** and give copies to other people.
- **Change it** to suit your needs.
- **Share your changed version** with others.
- **Sell copies**, including your changed version. Charging money is allowed.

## What you have to do if you share it

If you give someone a copy of Ballin' Roller, or a version you changed,
you must:

1. **Share the source code too.** Whoever gets your copy must be able to
   get the source code it was built from, so they can see how it works
   and change it themselves.
2. **Use this same license.** Your version must also be licensed under the
   GPLv3 (or a later version), so the freedoms keep going.
3. **Keep the copyright and license notices** that came with the program.
4. **Say what you changed.** If you modified the program, mark your
   changes and note when you made them.

You do not have to do any of this if you only change the program for your
own use and never give it to anyone else.
